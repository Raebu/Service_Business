# Electrical launch gate

The electrical service must not market itself as a live national network merely because applications are open.

## Initial thresholds

- 100 verified electrical businesses nationally before claiming meaningful national scale.
- At least 3 verified eligible providers in each activated service area.
- At least 95% historical or simulated fill rate in an area before normal consumer acquisition.
- Median match time at or below 15 minutes.

These are starting controls, not permanent commercial truth. They should be tuned from real service data.

## Area states

- `closed` — no eligible supply; no consumer acquisition.
- `recruiting` — provider recruitment active; no general customer marketing.
- `ready` — sufficient provider count but service KPIs not yet proven.
- `live` — provider depth and service KPIs satisfy the launch gate.

## Founding Electrician Programme

Before wider launch, recruit and verify founding electrical businesses. The programme should offer a public verification profile, website badge, early-area priority and participation in the operating standards. It must not imply accreditation by a statutory or independent external body.

## Location pages

Only create or index city/postcode service pages once that area is `live`. If an area falls below thresholds, paid acquisition should pause automatically and the supply-recruitment engine should prioritise that geography.
